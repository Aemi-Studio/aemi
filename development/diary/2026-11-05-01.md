# 2026-11-05 (01) — What the format parsers needed from aemi (ADR 0031, issue #5)

AemiHMMML's TOML parser was made faster than TOMLDecoder by profile, and most of the fixes were byte-level work AemiJSON and
AemiMarkdown had already done privately. Issue #5 listed ten items; nine are in this change (the tape utility waits for YAML).

- **C kernels**, as the owner asked: the complement set scan and bulk positions, NEON and x86 (SSE2/SSSE3/AVX2), each with its
  scalar reference, exhaustive and fuzz harness entries, threads, guard pages, and mutants (all killed).
- **SipHash** was checked against two independent implementations: OpenSSL's MAC for 2-4 under two keys, and CPython's `hash`
  (SipHash-1-3, zero key under `PYTHONHASHSEED=0`).
- **The decimal scanner** carries AemiJSON's careful parts over (padded zeros, the 19-digit edge, the long-decimal path) and adds
  separators and an eight-digit SWAR step. 40,000 generated literals round exactly as `Double(String)`.
- The 6.3.3 build could not be checked locally: swift.org's 6.3.3 toolchain cannot compile manifests against this Xcode
  (`unknown argument '-target-arch-variant'`); CI builds 6.3.3 on its own Xcode.
