# 2026-10-30 (02) — ADR 0020's kernels against MacFS's assembly, in MacFS

## Ledger

MacFS's `fskbench` (Swift, release, median of nine, Apple M2 Pro) put each aemi kernel beside MacFS's hand-written assembly, at the
sizes MacFS feeds them: whole extents, 4 KiB and 16 KiB blocks, and the 12-, 64- and 256-byte pieces of name hashes, group
descriptors and inodes.

| Claim | Probe | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| aemi's CRC is close enough to the assembly | 64 MiB and 4 KiB blocks | **REFUTED**: 52.4 and 50.4 against 59.1 and 57.4 GB/s |
| ... and on short pieces | 12 / 64 / 256 bytes | **REFUTED**: 2.07 / 9.54 / 33.6 against 4.61 / 12.2 / 43.4 |
| The short-piece gap is a fixed cost per call | per-call arithmetic: about 3 ns at every size | Holds: `adf_kernels_has_crc32_pmull()` (a `pthread_once` per call) and a byte-at-a-time tail. Every Apple arm64 target defines `__ARM_FEATURE_CRC32` and `__ARM_FEATURE_AES`, so the check is now compile-time there, and the tail takes one `crc32cw`/`ch`/`cb` step each: 4.60 / 12.3 / 45.5, level with or ahead of the assembly |
| The long-input gap is the compiler's schedule | the assembly orders `pmull2 acc` then `eor acc, acc, ...`, a pair Apple cores fuse; clang's order does not | Holds: SHA3's `veor3q_u64` (defined on every Apple arm64 target) joins both products and the block in one instruction: 59.1 GB/s on 64 MiB and 58.6 on 4 KiB, level with the assembly. The same `eor3` in the assembly gives nothing (58.7): it already had the fused pairs |
| aemi's bit count and UTF-16 narrowing hold up | same harness | Bit count level (60.3 against 60.2). UTF-16: 35 against 23 GB/s on a long run; a four-unit word step ends short names sooner, 8.05 against 6.69 GB/s at 12 units and 12.5 against 11.2 at 30 |
| A 27 GB/s UTF-16 reading was a regression | the same run with the word step reverted, and with the target moved by 1 KiB | **REFUTED**: unchanged without the step; a fresh build read 35 at both offsets. A build or layout state, not the kernel |

Each new step has a mutant that compiles and fails the tests (the four-, two-byte CRC steps, the word mask, a narrowing shift, the
`eor3` constants swapped). The first two `eor3` mutants left a variable unused and did not compile under the strict flags: a mutant
that does not build proves nothing, and was replaced.

## What it leaves

The vector fold now sits at the PMULL pipes' limit on this core. The next step is the hybrid that runs `crc32cx` streams beside it
(MacFS roadmap 0029).
