# 2026-10-30 (03) — CRC-32C: scalar streams beside the vector folds (MacFS roadmap 0029)

## Ledger

`scripts/checksum-kernels-check.sh --bench`, -Os, median of nine, Apple M2 Pro. "In cache" checksums the same block again and
again; "blocks" walks 64 MiB from DRAM.

| Claim | Probe | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| The fold's 59 GB/s is the kernel's limit | the fold alone on one block in cache | **REFUTED**: 67 to 70 in cache. 59 is what one core streams from DRAM here: every variant meets it |
| Three `crc32cx` streams beside the fold pay | corsix's `v9s3x2e` layout, one chunk [V \| S0 \| S1 \| S2], shift constants per region | Holds in cache: 87 to 90 against 67 to 70 |
| A large chunk is best | 64 KiB, 16 KiB and 4 KiB chunks | **REFUTED**: 90 and 92 in cache, but 43 and 38 from DRAM, where the streams lie kilobytes apart; 4 KiB holds the 59 from DRAM |
| A 4 KiB chunk costs its merge | 64 KiB in cache with 4 KiB chunks seeded one by the next: 78 | Holds; each chunk from 0, the running value shifted past it (`crc(c, B) = shift(c, \|B\|) ^ crc(0, B)`), lets chunks overlap: 90 |
| A tree merge shortens it further | three folds deep (16, 32, 64 bytes) against a chain of seven | **REFUTED**: equal on chunks, 4 to 8% slower on single 256-byte and 1 KiB inputs (throughput, not latency, bounds them). Kept as a chain |
| The non-Apple arm64 path builds | `--linux` (new): Alpine arm64 in `container`, clang and GCC, with and without the extensions on the command line | **REFUTED at first**: GCC needs `target("+crc")` where clang took `"crc"` (clang takes both), and `adf_cpu.c` needs `linux-headers` for `asm/hwcap.h`. Fixed; 60,000 checks each, 0 failures |

Every hybrid part has a mutant that the new chunk-boundary test fails (48 of 48 checks): each of four shift constants, the
streams' last words, the order of the combination, a stream reading its first word twice.
